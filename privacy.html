Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · 中文版

This policy explains how 叮咚News ("the app") handles data. The app is a private tool that its owner, GreatCName, runs on their own Mac to publish their own videos to their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no server of its own, and does not collect data about anyone else.

1. Data the app accesses

From Google (YouTube API Services). When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app asks for these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the channel.
  • youtube: manage the channel's videos, for example set a thumbnail, check processing and privacy status, or delete a video the owner asked the app to remove.
  • youtube.readonly: read the channel's ID, name, handle and profile picture, so the owner can tell connected channels apart.

The app also receives OAuth access and refresh tokens, and the ID, link and status of each video it uploads.

From Meta. When the owner connects a Facebook Page or Instagram professional account, the app asks for permission to list the owner's Pages, publish posts and videos to them, and publish to the linked Instagram account. It receives the Page or account ID, name, picture and access tokens.

Files the owner adds. Videos, thumbnails and the text the owner writes or approves, plus frames and a speech transcript the app makes from each video on the owner's computer.

2. How the data is used

Only to do what the owner asks: upload and schedule videos, set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, check whether a video was published correctly, delete a video when the owner asks, and show the owner which accounts are connected. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Where the data is stored

  • Everything is stored in a local database on the owner's Mac. There is no cloud copy and no server operated by the app.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved. The encryption key is kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • The app's interface only listens on the owner's own computer.

4. Sharing

The data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. It leaves the owner's computer only in these cases, each one needed for a feature the owner turned on:

  • Google / YouTube and Meta receive the videos and text the owner publishes, through their official APIs.
  • The owner's own Telegram bot receives notifications, which can include a channel or Page name, a video title and the video's link.
  • An AI service chosen by the owner (DeepSeek or Anthropic) receives frames and the speech transcript of the owner's own video, together with the owner's style notes, to draft titles and captions. No data received from Google APIs is sent to an AI service: no tokens, channel information or YouTube data.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

叮咚News's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features described above.
  • It is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • It is not used for advertising, and no person other than the owner reads it.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner also agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app, or deletes the app's data folder on their computer. Disconnecting removes the stored tokens.
  • Google access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page (Google security settings).
  • Facebook and Instagram access can be removed in Facebook's settings, under "Business integrations".

Data deletion requests. Because all data stays on the owner's computer, deleting the account in the app or deleting its data folder removes it completely. You can also email vancooler99@gmail.com to ask for deletion; requests are answered within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact
