叮咚News

A personal video publishing tool

叮咚News is a private, self-hosted tool that its owner runs on their own Mac. It publishes short videos the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channel and other social media accounts. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no hosted service, and no advertising.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Contact: vancooler99@gmail.com

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel, right away or at a scheduled time, with a title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Publishes to the owner's Facebook Page and Instagram professional account, when the owner connects them.
  • Checks whether each upload was processed and whether it is public, and notifies the owner through the owner's own Telegram bot.
  • Drafts titles, descriptions and tags from the video's frames and speech, using an AI service the owner chooses.

How it uses your Google account

The owner signs in with Google to let the tool upload and manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel and read the channel's name and picture. The tool uses YouTube API Services. Everything it receives is stored only on the owner's computer, and access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
